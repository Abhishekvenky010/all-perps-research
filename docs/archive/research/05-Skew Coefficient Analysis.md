## Skew Coefficient Analysis

Experiment:
Fixed market imbalance of 80% LONG exposure.

Variable:
skewCoefficient

Results:

| Coefficient | Price Impact |
|-------------|--------------|
| 0.05        | 4%           |
| 0.2         | 16%          |
| 0.5         | 40%          |
| 1           | 80%          |

Observation:

Increasing skewCoefficient makes the AMM more aggressive against directional imbalance.
However, excessively high values reduce market usability.
