# Checkpoint 7.5 — Self-Skew Settlement Fix

## Production change

Canonical settlement pricing now requires the OPEN position being settled.
`getSettlementPrice` validates it, subtracts exactly its stored size from the
matching side of a copied market state, rejects non-finite or negative
counterfactual OI, and applies the existing canonical mark formula to that
copy. It does not mutate the market, position, or vault.

**Before:** the settlement mark included the closing position's OI.

**After:** the settlement mark excludes only the closing position; all other
open positions continue to contribute to skew.

Voluntary close calculates this mark before settlement commit and OI release.
Single liquidation uses the same helper. The liquidation sweep evaluates each
position at its own-exposure-excluded mark; batch liquidation derives each
price from the same pre-sweep market snapshot.

Entry pricing remains `getAverageExecutionPrice()` and was not changed.
Pricing formulas, `skewCoefficient`, `capacityCoefficient`, `maxCapacity`,
TWAP, capacity, vault, margin, liquidation thresholds, and oracle behavior
were not changed.

undefined, not a finite ratio claimed as proof of security.
